About Our Clinic

Everything your skin, body and hair need — under one roof, and under one medical standard.

Who We Are

Aesthetic medicine, practised as medicine

Hollywood Clinic is an aesthetic and dermatology centre in Heliopolis, Cairo. We are not a beauty salon that added a laser. Every treatment here is assessed, planned and performed by a licensed physician.

What We Offer You

Most people arrive with a concern, not a device name

So that is how we work. Tell us what bothers you, and we will tell you what actually treats it.

For your face and skin

Fine lines and wrinkles · lost volume and definition · dull, tired skin · acne and acne scarring · pigmentation and melasma · enlarged pores and texture · sagging along the jaw and neck.

Treated with: anti-wrinkle injections, dermal fillers, collagen biostimulators, radiofrequency microneedling, ultrasound tightening, radiofrequency skin treatments, medical facials and skin boosters — chosen for your skin, not from a menu.

For your body

Stubborn fat that will not move · cellulite · skin that loosened after weight loss or pregnancy · weak muscle tone · body proportions you would like rebalanced.

Treated with: muscle stimulation, fat freezing, cellulite treatment, radiofrequency body contouring and skin tightening — and, where it matters more than any device, a clinical nutrition plan built alongside them.

For your hair

Thinning, shedding, postpartum loss and a receding front line.

Treated with: regenerative scalp therapies and growth-factor treatments.

For unwanted hair

Laser hair removal — safe across every skin tone, with built-in cooling and no downtime.

Treated with: medical-grade laser hair removal.

For intimate wellness

Non-surgical intimate treatments — in complete privacy, with a female physician.

Treated with: radiofrequency intimate wellness treatment.

What makes the difference here

Dermatology, plastic surgery and clinical nutrition sit under one roof. A woman concerned about her body is assessed from all three perspectives at once — not sent between clinics collecting contradictory opinions.

2

A doctor performs your treatment

Not a technician, not an assistant. The physician who assesses you is the one who treats you.

3

Your consultation is free, and it is not a sales call

We work towards a face that looks rested, not reworked. A result nobody can name, but everyone notices.

5

Equipment we can defend

Original devices from the manufacturers who made them — not the cheaper copy.

Your plan is yours
