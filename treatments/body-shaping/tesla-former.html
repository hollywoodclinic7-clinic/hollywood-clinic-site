What is Tesla Former?

Tesla Former is an advanced body sculpting device that uses functional electromagnetic stimulation (FMS) to make muscles contract intensively — far beyond what voluntary exercise can produce. That is why it is known as the gym alternative. The device is manufactured by Iskra Medical in Europe. A session runs from 30 to 60 minutes according to the area treated, and triggers around 50,000 supramaximal muscle contractions in that area. These contractions force the muscles to adapt and rebuild stronger, denser, and more defined — while simultaneously stimulating fat burning in the surrounding tissue. The result is the same biological effect as months of intense training, condensed into a short course of sessions. Tesla Former is non-invasive, completely needle-free, and requires zero effort from you. You simply lie down on the device, and the electromagnetic stimulation does the work. There is no pain, no sweating, no clothes to change, and no recovery time. It is widely used in Europe and the Middle East as a premium body sculpting solution.

Contraindications

Safety first

Tesla Former is not suitable in the following cases. A full medical history and a metal-implant screening are always done first.

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemakers or any implanted electronic medical device
  • Metal implants in the treatment area (hip replacement, surgical screws, plates, IUDs in the abdominal area)
  • Drug delivery devices (insulin pumps, etc.) in the treatment area
  • Active malignancy / cancer
  • Severe heart, lung, or neurological conditions — requires medical clearance
  • Epilepsy or history of seizures
  • Hernias in the treatment area
  • Recent surgery in the area
  • Fever or acute infection
  • Bleeding or clotting disorders
  • Severe uncontrolled diabetes

Treatment comparisons

Tesla Former vs. Other Treatments

Tesla Former vs. Clatuu Alpha (Cryolipolysis)

Clatuu Alpha targets fat only — it freezes and permanently destroys fat cells, but does nothing for muscle. Tesla Former builds muscle AND reduces fat in the same area. Best combination: Clatuu Alpha to eliminate stubborn fat pockets, then Tesla Former to add muscle definition underneath.

Tesla Former vs. Body FX

Body FX uses RF + vacuum + electrical pulses for fat reduction and skin tightening. Tesla Former focuses on muscle building. They address completely different goals — body shape (Body FX) vs. muscle definition (Tesla Former). Used together they create comprehensive sculpting.

Tesla Former vs. EMTONE

EMTONE is designed for cellulite reduction and surface skin smoothing. Tesla Former is for muscle building and deeper sculpting. They're often combined in a complete program — Tesla Former for the muscle, EMTONE for the surface.

Tesla Former vs. Traditional Gym Training

The gym uses voluntary muscle activation — typically activating 30–40% of muscle fibers per exercise. Tesla Former produces near-100% activation through supramaximal contractions. It's not a replacement for the gym for cardiovascular fitness or athletic performance — but for pure muscle building and definition in specific areas, it's significantly more efficient per minute.

Tesla Former vs. Brazilian Butt Lift Surgery (BBL)

BBL is surgery — fat transferred from other body areas to the glutes for dramatic shape change. Tesla Former is non-surgical — it lifts and tones the glutes through muscle building. Different goals: BBL for volume change, Tesla Former for shape and tone without surgery.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Tesla Former hurt?

No. The contractions feel intense and unusual — like very strong muscle squeezing — but not painful. Intensity is adjusted to your tolerance and you stay in full control during the session.

How long is each session?

From 30 to 60 minutes, according to the area treated and how many areas you do in one visit.

How many sessions do I need?

A typical course is 6 to 10 sessions, done 1 to 2 times per week. Most patients start seeing results within 1 to 2 months, and the result keeps developing for several weeks after the course is finished.

Can I exercise the same day?

Yes. There's no recovery period required, but some patients prefer to rest after intense Tesla Former sessions because the muscles have been heavily worked.

Will I lose weight with Tesla Former?

Tesla Former is a body sculpting treatment, not a weight loss treatment. You may see a small reduction in fat in treated areas, but the main effect is muscle building and definition. For weight loss, a structured nutrition plan is the right approach — Dr. Sara, Dr. Rana Ibrahim, or Dr. Ehssan can build one for you.

Is Tesla Former safe?

Yes — Tesla Former is a CE-marked medical device with documented clinical safety. Side effects are mild and temporary (muscle soreness similar to after a workout). Strict contraindications apply for patients with metal implants, pacemakers, or pregnancy.

Will my muscle definition disappear if I stop?

As with gym training, muscle gains gradually reduce if you stop maintenance. Most patients schedule periodic maintenance sessions (every 1–2 months) to preserve their results, or combine Tesla Former with regular exercise for sustained tone.

Is Tesla Former better than EMSculpt?

They use the same fundamental technology (functional electromagnetic stimulation / supramaximal contractions) and produce similar results. Differences lie in device design, applicator options, and protocols. Both are considered premium FMS devices. Hollywood Clinic uses authentic Tesla Former.
